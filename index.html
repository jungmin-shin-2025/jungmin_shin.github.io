Jungmin Shin

I am a sociologist studying gender, work, and family. I am a Ph.D. candidate at University at Albany (SUNY). I use quantitative methods and administrative data to study how occupational structures, family arrangements, and public policies produce inequalities in paid and unpaid work.

My work has been published in The Sociological Quarterly, Journal of Ethnic and Migration Studies, and Journals of Gerontology. I am the recipient of Sage College Publishing Kieth Roberts Teaching Innovations Award.
